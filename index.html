01The Farm

For the better part of three decades, Old MacDonald's Farm was one of the Okanagan's best-loved roadside stops — an old-fashioned family amusement park and petting zoo right on Highway 97 in Westbank (today West Kelowna). Generations of local kids and vacationing families came for the farm animals, the little train, the fishing pond, and the waterslides next door.

It stood at 3020 Louie Drive, on the land where the Real Canadian Superstore sits today. Travel writers of the era described it as the perfect outing for young kids — farm animals to visit, a miniature train, tractors to climb on, dollar pony rides, and free strollers for the littlest visitors. It was also a regular school field-trip destination.

In the fall of 1999, after an illness swept through the animal pens, the farm closed its gates for the last time. A few years later the land was redeveloped into the shopping centre that stands there now — but the memories never left the valley.

02Attractions

Ask anyone who grew up in the Central Okanagan in the 70s, 80s or 90s and they'll rattle off the highlights like it was yesterday:

  • Petting zoo & baby animals — farm animals and bottle-fed babies you could hold
  • Omar the camel — the farm's most famous resident
  • Pony rides — around the paddock, plus pony-drawn carts
  • Hayrides & wagon rides — horse-drawn rides around the grounds
  • The beetle train — the children's ladybug/caterpillar train, plus a little red commuter train
  • Fishing pond — a stocked pond where you could catch rainbow trout from the bridge (certificates for your first fish!)
  • Whale waterslide & kiddie pool — a big whale standing in the middle of the pool, with two waterslides coming out of its mouth: a left slide and a right slide
  • Frog sprinklers — a splash pad of big frog statues spraying water, with the shooting-gallery barn behind
  • Upstairs art gallery — Indigenous art and crafts above the barn
  • Arcade & games room — pool table, ping pong, foosball and other table games
  • Costumed mascots — a chicken and a pig wandering the grounds, posing for photos with the kids
  • The big roadside sign — the cartoon EIEIO sign that waved travellers in off Highway 97

03Timeline

  • 1970s–80sThe Martens family lives right on the farm; their dad keeps the place running as maintenance man.
  • June 16, 1989A young visitor earns a "First Fish" certificate for a rainbow trout caught at the pond.
  • June 1991Children pile into the farm's little red commuter train — a moment preserved by the Westbank Museum and Archives.
  • Fall 1999After an illness spreads through the animal pens, Old MacDonald's Farm closes for good.
  • Dec 2004Anthem Properties advances plans for a 350,000-sq-ft shopping centre on the old farm site, anchored by Canadian Tire and the Real Canadian Superstore.
  • TodayThe Superstore at 3020 Louie Drive stands where the barn, the pond and the beetle train used to be.
